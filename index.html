03

Live Games — Agent 7’s Menu

Agent 7 works LIVE ONLY. When Glam goes live on TikTok, it watches her live chat for the window she picks — the last 20 minutes is standard — pulls the real people engaging (recent commenters and her Top 10 Engagers), and builds one of these games from those people for her to play on the live. Shares, taps, likes and gifts count only when TikTok actually shows them: anything it can’t see is marked NOT VISIBLE, and a pull that stops early is labeled PARTIAL with its times. No invented names, no invented numbers — ever.

GAME 01

Names Wheel

Everybody who commented in the window goes on the wheel. Glam spins it on the live and the name it lands on wins.

GAME 02

Number Wheel 1 to 200

Viewers guess a number in the comments — Agent 7 logs each name with their guess — then the 1-to-200 wheel spins for the winning number.

GAME 03

Top 10 Engagers Wheel

Only the Top 10 Engagers from the window earn a spot on this wheel. The hardest-working viewers play for the prize.

GAME 04

Top Engager Showdown

The top engagers face off in rounds, head to head, until one champion is left standing.

GAME 05

Trivia Pop

Agent 7 writes a question about the live’s topic. The first right answer in the comments takes it.

GAME 06

Last One Standing

The wheel spins to eliminate one name at a time. The last name left on the wheel is the winner — pure suspense.

GAME 07

Mystery Pumpkin Pick

The winner picks a pumpkin and reveals the mystery prize hiding inside it. The prizes are stocked below — real ones, Glam-approved.

GAME 08

Secret Word Drop

Agent 7 drops a secret word into the live. The first person to comment that exact word wins.

GAME 09

Closest Guess

Viewers guess a number in the comments. Closest guess without going over wins — no spin needed.

GAME 10

Head-to-Head

Two top engagers, one wheel, one spin. Winner takes it — the fastest showdown on the menu.

08

The Farm Laws

The rules every agent obeys on every single run — Shop, content, lives, all of it. No exceptions, no shortcuts — this is what keeps the accounts safe and the videos believable.

🎯

Exact products only

Every scene is anchored to the listing’s real product photo. If the render doesn’t match the real product, it fails QC — look-alikes never ship.

🚫

No prices. No sold counts.

Never spoken, never on screen, never in captions. Prices change and a stale price is a violation risk — the product link always shows the live one.

✂️

No dead air

Edits are tightened so there are no long pauses — natural breath beats only. The video moves the way a real person talks.

👁️

It has to feel human

Conversational delivery, natural gestures and head movement, normal blinking. If it looks like a robot, it goes back for a rebuild.

🏷️

AI label on, product attached

Every post carries the AI-generated label and the real product link, with the caption scrubbed clean before it goes out.

🔍

Director QC before anything posts

Frames are swept, sound is checked, the product is matched against the listing. A video that fails never reaches the schedule.

🎃

Live games: real people, real prizes, Glam’s call

Agent 7 works only from people actually seen on the live — never an invented name or number. Prizes are only the prizes Glam listed, and winners and prize messages always wait for Glam’s approval of the exact text before anything is sent.

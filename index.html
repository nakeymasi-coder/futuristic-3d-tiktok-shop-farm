Live Games — Agent 7’s Menu

Agent 7 works LIVE ONLY. When Glam goes live on TikTok, it watches her live chat for the window she picks — the last 20 minutes is standard — pulls the real people engaging (recent commenters and her Top 10 Engagers), and builds one of these games from those people for her to play on the live. Shares, taps, likes and gifts count only when TikTok actually shows them: anything it can’t see is marked NOT VISIBLE, and a pull that stops early is labeled PARTIAL with its times. No invented names, no invented numbers — ever.

GAME 01

Names Wheel

Everybody who commented in the window goes on the wheel. Glam spins it on the live and the name it lands on wins.

GAME 02

Number Wheel 1 to 200

Viewers guess a number in the comments — Agent 7 logs each name with their guess — then the 1-to-200 wheel spins for the winning number.

GAME 03

Top 10 Engagers Wheel

Only the Top 10 Engagers from the window earn a spot on this wheel. The hardest-working viewers play for the prize.

GAME 04

Top Engager Showdown

The top engagers face off in rounds, head to head, until one champion is left standing.

GAME 05

Trivia Pop

Agent 7 writes a question about the live’s topic. The first right answer in the comments takes it.

GAME 06

Last One Standing

The wheel spins to eliminate one name at a time. The last name left on the wheel is the winner — pure suspense.

GAME 07

Mystery Pumpkin Pick

The winner picks a pumpkin and reveals the mystery prize hiding inside it. The prizes are stocked below — real ones, Glam-approved.

GAME 08

Secret Word Drop

Agent 7 drops a secret word into the live. The first person to comment that exact word wins.

GAME 09

Closest Guess

Viewers guess a number in the comments. Closest guess without going over wins — no spin needed.

GAME 10

Head-to-Head

Two top engagers, one wheel, one spin. Winner takes it — the fastest showdown on the menu.

🎃 Mystery Pumpkin Pick — the prizes inside

These are the only prizes stocked in the pumpkins — the ones Glam approved. The pumpkin only shows the prize on the live: the real code or product goes to the winner after, and only after Glam approves the exact message.

Product-to-Post Workflow

The farm’s fashion & accessory lane for TikTok Shop — and the detail for steps 7 and 8 of the Run Order up top, not a separate order of its own. One product at a time, one image at a time, in this exact order. The sequence below is non-negotiable: nothing jumps ahead, ever.

RUN ORDER 7

One styled image

Feed the actual product photo in as the reference image → generate ONE styled model image — a fresh model per GG casting. Never describe the product in words in the prompt, no label wording at all, and keep the scene line short — “woman in a kitchen holding the bottle” is enough. The longer the prompt, the more the model improvises.

RUN ORDER 7 · EDIT LOOP

Review + edit loop

Review the image and edit the existing image until it’s approved. Never jump ahead — no script, no caption while the image is still being fixed.

RUN ORDER 8

Say “Next”

“Next” delivers all three at once: the Video Express clip prompts (C## format, Realistic Consistent Character workflow, the approved styled image and the real listing photo as the references, every clip 8 seconds flat, max 8 spoken words in a clip — four clips make the video) + the TikTok hook + the ready-to-post caption. Step 8 carries no product description in words and no label wording either — the photos do the product work, and each clip’s scene line stays short.

BACK TO RUN ORDER 1

New product

A new product restarts the whole run at Run Order step 1 — fresh product photo, fresh model, fresh image.

PRODUCT RECEIVED→ IMAGE CREATED→ IMAGE REVISION LOOP→ IMAGE APPROVED→ NEXT→ VIDEO EXPRESS CLIPS + HOOK + CAPTION→ NEW PRODUCT
The “Next” rule: “Next” never means make another image. It means the image is approved and it’s time for the Video Express clip prompts + hook + caption package. And Step 8 follows the same prompt rule as Step 7: the clip prompts feed the approved image and the real listing photo in as references — they never describe the product in words, they carry no label wording, and the scene line stays short. CASTING

Fresh face, every product

  • Adult Black women, generally early 30s — late 30s and 40s included, with real size diversity.
  • A fresh rotating model every product: different face, skin tone, body type and hairstyle — never the same model twice.
  • Natural light, phone-camera feel — like a friend shot it, not a studio ad.
  • Full figure, head-to-toe, and the product is never obscured.

Plain and simple: this lane uses rotating models on purpose — it does not use the farm’s locked character.

PRODUCT FIDELITY

The product is the hero

  • Feed the actual product photo in as the reference image itself. Never describe the product in words in the prompt and hope the picture comes back right — it won’t. The photo carries the product; the words don’t.
  • No label wording in the prompt at all — no brand name, no label text. Describing the product in words is where the morphing and the invented lettering come from.
  • Keep the scene line short — “woman in a kitchen holding the bottle” is enough. The longer the prompt gets, the more the model improvises.
  • The reference photo is the authority — if the image and the photo disagree, the photo wins.
  • Detail unclear? Never invent a claim about it.
  • An AI model is a creative demonstration — never presented as a real buyer testimonial.
THE “NEXT” PACKAGE

What “Next” delivers — all three, together

A

Video Express clip prompts — C## format

  • C## format — one prompt per clip, Realistic Consistent Character workflow
  • Approved styled image and real listing photo as the references — Consistent Character ON. The photos do the product work: no product description in words, no label wording anywhere in a Step 8 clip prompt
  • Scene line short only — one short line per clip, e.g. “woman in a kitchen holding the bottle.” Long prompts make the model improvise
  • Every clip is 8 seconds flat, max 8 spoken words in a clip — four clips make the video, natural movement, lip sync
  • Ends with a 1 to 2 second clean close-up of the REAL listing photo — that close-up is what protects the label
  • No music, and no text or subtitles in the Video Express video
B

One scroll-stopping hook

One line that stops the thumb — and it never repeats the dialogue from the Video Express clip prompts. The hook and the script say different things.

C

Ready-to-post caption

Written and ready to paste, ending with a comment-driving question so viewers answer back. Hashtags optional.

The Farm Laws

The rules every agent obeys on every single run — Shop, content, lives, all of it. No exceptions, no shortcuts — this is what keeps the accounts safe and the videos believable.

🎯

Exact products only

Every scene is anchored to the listing’s real product photo — fed in as the reference image itself. Prompts never describe the product in words, carry no label wording, and keep the scene line short. If the render doesn’t match the real product, it fails QC — look-alikes never ship.

🚫

No prices. No sold counts.

Never spoken, never on screen, never in captions. Prices change and a stale price is a violation risk — the product link always shows the live one.

✂️

No dead air

Edits are tightened so there are no long pauses — natural breath beats only. The video moves the way a real person talks.

👁️

It has to feel human

Conversational delivery, natural gestures and head movement, normal blinking. If it looks like a robot, it goes back for a rebuild.

🏷️

AI label on, product attached

Every post carries the AI-generated label and the real product link, with the caption scrubbed clean before it goes out.

🔍

Quality control before anything posts

Mila is in charge of quality control — she checks each step at its gate and frame-checks the finished video against the listing, then Glam gives the final verdict. Indigo builds and hands off only; she never checks her own work. A video that fails never reaches the schedule.

🎃

Live games: real people, real prizes, Glam’s call

Agent 7 works only from people actually seen on the live — never an invented name or number. Prizes are only the prizes Glam listed, and winners and prize messages always wait for Glam’s approval of the exact text before anything is sent.

👗

Product-to-Post lane: the sequence is law

Image first, edits until approved, “Next” delivers Video Express clip prompts + hook + caption, never out of order. Every clip is 8 seconds flat, max 8 spoken words in a clip — four clips make the video — and it ends with a 1 to 2 second clean close-up of the REAL listing photo.

✅

QC gate between every step

Every step passes quality control before the next step starts. Mila checks — Mila is in charge of quality control; Indigo builds and hands off only and never checks her own work. Fail = sent back to the agent who did it, with what’s wrong written down. The run does not move forward on a fail — no fixing it forward, no skipping, no marking done on a promise. And no story sentence from the Scout means no handoff and no script.

🛑

Can’t use it? Stop and report BLOCKED

If any agent can’t use something a farm step needs, STOP immediately and report BLOCKED: [what] - [why] to Glam. No substitute tools, no outside sites, no workarounds, never go off on your own.

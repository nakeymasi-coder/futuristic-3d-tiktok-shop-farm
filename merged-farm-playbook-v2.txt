03 Live Games — Agent 7’s Menu
Agent 7 works LIVE ONLY. When Glam goes live on TikTok, it watches her live chat for the window she picks — the last 20 minutes is standard — pulls the real people engaging (recent commenters and her Top 10 Engagers), and builds one of these games from those people for her to play on the live. Shares, taps, likes and gifts count only when TikTok actually shows them: anything it can’t see is marked NOT VISIBLE, and a pull that stops early is labeled PARTIAL with its times. No invented names, no invented numbers — ever.
GAME 01
Names Wheel
Everybody who commented in the window goes on the wheel. Glam spins it on the live and the name it lands on wins.
GAME 02
Number Wheel 1 to 200
Viewers guess a number in the comments — Agent 7 logs each name with their guess — then the 1-to-200 wheel spins for the winning number.
GAME 03
Top 10 Engagers Wheel
Only the Top 10 Engagers from the window earn a spot on this wheel. The hardest-working viewers play for the prize.
GAME 04
Top Engager Showdown
The top engagers face off in rounds, head to head, until one champion is left standing.
GAME 05
Trivia Pop
Agent 7 writes a question about the live’s topic. The first right answer in the comments takes it.
GAME 06
Last One Standing
The wheel spins to eliminate one name at a time. The last name left on the wheel is the winner — pure suspense.
GAME 07
Mystery Pumpkin Pick
The winner picks a pumpkin and reveals the mystery prize hiding inside it. The prizes are stocked below — real ones, Glam-approved.
GAME 08
Secret Word Drop
Agent 7 drops a secret word into the live. The first person to comment that exact word wins.
GAME 09
Closest Guess
Viewers guess a number in the comments. Closest guess without going over wins — no spin needed.
GAME 10
Head-to-Head
Two top engagers, one wheel, one spin. Winner takes it — the fastest showdown on the menu.

PTP Product-to-Post Workflow
The farm’s fashion & accessory lane for TikTok Shop — and the detail for steps 5 and 6 of the Run Order up top, not a separate order of its own. One product at a time, one image at a time, in this exact order. The sequence below is non-negotiable: nothing jumps ahead, ever.
RUN ORDER 5
One styled image
Upload the product image → generate ONE styled model image — a fresh model per GG casting. Just one — the product on a model, nothing else yet.
RUN ORDER 5 · EDIT LOOP
Review + edit loop
Review the image and edit the existing image until it’s approved. Never jump ahead — no script, no caption while the image is still being fixed.
RUN ORDER 6
Say “Next”
“Next” delivers all three at once: the Video Express clip prompts (C## format, Realistic Consistent Character workflow, approved styled image as the only reference, max 12 spoken words per line) + the TikTok hook + the ready-to-post caption.
BACK TO RUN ORDER 1
New product
A new product restarts the whole run at Run Order step 1 — fresh product photo, fresh model, fresh image.

PART 2 — GG SKILL (TikTok Shop Product-to-Post Workflow)
# TikTok Shop Product-to-Post Workflow — farm lane (TikTok Farm)

White-label workflow (all original branding removed at Glam's direction, 2026-11-15). Full sellable guide: ~/workspace/your_files/tiktok-shop-product-to-post-workflow/GUIDE.md. Runs in chat, inside the TikTok Farm chat, as its own lane for Glam's TikTok Shop fashion/accessory products.

Mission: turn a product reference into differentiated, natural-looking TikTok Shop fashion content — product accurate, video sounding like a real conversation, not a repetitive commercial.

## The non-negotiable sequence (state machine — follow exactly)

PRODUCT RECEIVED → IMAGE CREATED → IMAGE REVISION LOOP → IMAGE APPROVED → "NEXT" → VIDEO EXPRESS CLIP PROMPTS + HOOK + CAPTION → NEW PRODUCT
(Google Flow replaced by Video Express — Glam's decision, Nov 23.)

1. Glam uploads product image(s) → generate ONE styled model image first. Standalone, no text, no collage (unless multiple people / a comparison is explicitly requested). No script or caption at this stage unless she explicitly asks.
2. Glam reviews and requests edits → edit the EXISTING image, surgically, until approved. Never jump ahead.
3. Glam says "Next" (or "love it" / "yes" / "that's it" followed by Next) → the image is approved; "Next" NEVER means make another image. Deliver all three, in order, with clear headings: (A) the Video Express clip prompts (C## format, Realistic Consistent Character workflow, approved styled image as the only reference, max 12 spoken words per line), (B) TikTok hook, (C) ready-to-post caption. Do not shorten to one line.
4. New product uploaded → restart at Step 1.

Activation: she uploads a product photo and says "do your thing" / "put it to work" / asks for a styled look; or asks for a different model, hair, pose, styling, setting, or accurate product details; or types "Next" after an approved image.

## Model casting and visual DNA

- Adult African American/Black women, generally early 30s; intentionally include late 30s/40s and size diversity when relevant.
- ROTATE skin tones, faces, body types, hair textures, cuts, colors, poses, shoes, locations. Never default to the same model or the same blonde highlighted updo. (This lane deliberately uses a fresh model per product — confirmed by Glam 2026-11-15, "keep hers": it does NOT use the farm's locked Character Glam.)
- Use the listing's actual size range to inform styling; include curvy, slim-thick, and plus-size figures where appropriate; never imply an item fits a size not offered; never slim a plus-size figure down.
- Fresh aesthetic per item: neighborhood fair, city sidewalk, rooftop, brunch spot, bedroom, living room, porch, party venue, casual street, fall market.
- Natural window/existing outdoor light, plausible phone-camera perspective, visible texture, realistic anatomy, non-studio staging. Framing follows the category (see the casting rule in Compliance); primary product clearly visible, never obscured by hair, accessories, bags, or poses.

## Product fidelity rules (the product is the hero)

- Preserve correct color, print, logo/lettering, garment type, silhouette, hemline, closures, buttons, cutouts, pockets, laces, hardware, material appearance — exactly as the reference shows.
- Never add features that don't exist or omit defining ones (show the cardigan's buttons; keep the boot's zipper and sole; keep the jumpsuit's straps and patchwork).
- Shoes/bags: frame the accessory prominently, style the outfit around it. Garments: fabric and fit stay visible.
- Two-product variation (tee vs sweatshirt, two colors of one jacket): distinct models in their respective REAL versions; one scene with two friends allowed when requested.
- The reference image is the authoritative design source. Detail unclear → do not invent a definitive feature or performance claim.
- Never claim an AI model purchased, tried on, wore, or personally tested the item as a real customer. Creative character dialogue only — never presented as genuine firsthand proof.
- Image quality gate: standalone portrait, ideally 9:16; no collage/graphics/text overlays/watermarks/app controls; different face + distinctive hairstyle from the prior product (unless she asks to retain a character); expressive eyes visible; credible fingers/limbs; realistic fabric fit and drape; all product-defining details intact and easy to see; shoes visible when shoes are the item.

## Edit feedback — literal and surgical

- "Different scenery" → change location only. "Different model" → replace face/body identity, vary styling where permitted, keep the exact product. "Change hairstyle" → hair only. "The buttons are missing" → correct count/placement from the reference. "Keep her but show the back" → same person/outfit/setting, new angle, accurate back details.
- If she changes an item detail or scene, revise only what she asked; preserve everything already approved.

## The "Next" package — three deliverables

### A — Video Express clip prompts
- One copy-ready detailed prompt; the approved image is the strict scene, character, and product reference.
- Location, exact look, lighting, featured item, then a simple natural movement sequence planned as 0–3s / 3–6s / 6–8s (every clip is 8 seconds flat).
- Dialogue: every clip is 8 seconds flat and carries max 12 spoken words; a line that needs more words is split across clips, never stretched. Four clips make the video. (Clip rule, Glam + Claude, Nov 24 — the old 10-second shape and its 18–27 words are retired.)
- Product is the visual focus: highlight one or two observable details, not the whole outfit.
- Voice: lively, Southern, grown-woman, occasionally funny, confident, conversational; vary phrases. No robotic sales voice; no repetitive "giving thick" / "baby" / "I came for…" formulas.
- One continuous shot, subtle natural ambience, believable fabric movement and expressions, proper lip sync, NO background music, NO text/subtitles/captions, no flashy camera effects.
- Two women: identify positions, voices, styles, exact speaker turns; no overlapping dialogue; each line fits its own 8-second clip, max 12 spoken words.
- Skeleton: "Use approved reference image. Create a realistic 8-second phone-recorded video of [CHARACTER] in [LOCATION], wearing [EXACT PRODUCT AND STYLING]. 0–3s: [natural introductory action]. 3–6s: [demonstrate key product feature]. 6–8s: [finish and reaction]. Dialogue: "[ONE SHORT, DISTINCTIVE, NATURAL LINE]." Preserve product and character continuity; realistic lip sync, one continuous shot, natural ambient light and sound. No music, text overlay, captions, or subtitles."

### B — TikTok hook
One short scroll-stopping opening line specific to the product or scene — playful, relatable, funny, or a style dilemma. Do not repeat the spoken dialogue verbatim.

### C — Ready-to-post caption
Short-to-medium, conversational: main item, key visible details, styling or occasion, and a natural question that encourages comments. Subtle CTA like "check the linked product" is fine; never force "tap the cart" or "I got this from TikTok Shop" into every caption. Hashtags optional — omit entirely when Glam asks for hashtag-free; if used, a few audience/product-specific ones, no generic trend stuffing.

## Dialogue playbook — one clear angle per product

Personality: relatable big-sister energy, Southern humor, confidence, a little hood flair, authentic grown-woman conversation. Rotate angles: Practical (pockets, layering, versatility, styling with jeans/boots) · Humorous (errands outfit turns brunch-ready; going out just to show the jacket) · Confidence (statement dress, bold color, playful reveal) · Conversational (two friends debating tee vs sweatshirt / two jacket colors) · Fit education (demonstrate drape, rise, sleeve length, shape — never unverified softness, support, stretch, or warmth claims).
Sample lines (for voice only): "Now this little jacket? The sleeves sold me. Jeans, boots, and I'm good to go." / "I like the tee version." "And I want my sweatshirt!" "Same design, different plans." / "These pockets are doing their job! I kept the rest simple so the pants could shine."
Never: claim "true to size" / "perfect" / "stretches so well" without reliable product evidence; assign an invented height, weight, or purchase history to a supposed real shopper (AI fit demos label illustrative measurements in the caption and reference the seller size chart); imply authenticity, availability, discount, or stock urgency without verification; present AI renderings of logos as proof of authenticity.

## TikTok Shop execution and compliance

- Promotional/commercial content disclosure on, and attach the ACTUAL correct product listing.
- AI-generated model = creative demonstration, not a buyer testimonial; make the required synthetic/AI content disclosure (farm law: AI label ON).
- Check product title, size chart, price, availability, and claims in the current seller listing before publishing — verification only.
- Music added later must be cleared for commercial/promotional use; Video Express source videos stay music-free.
- No unsupported review language, guaranteed fit, false scarcity, or material promises.

## Glam's farm laws layered on top (win on any conflict)

- NO prices and NO sold counts in any video or caption — ever (false-advertising/violation protection). Prices are checked for picking/verification only and never spoken, shown, or captioned.
- Nothing posts automatically. The "Next" package is delivered to Glam in chat; posting happens only after her separate approval, with the correct product attached — never post without the product link.
- Exact products only: the listing's real photo is the reference; look-alikes fail QC.
- Mila's QC, then Glam, before anything posts.

## Final quality gate (run before delivering each stage)

Correct workflow stage? Product hero clear? Model genuinely different from the last? Product reference faithful? For "Next": all three deliverables present, every clip 8 seconds flat with max 12 spoken words a line, no embedded music/text, natural conversational tone, caption ready to post, hashtag preference honored, no unsupported claims or fake testimonials?
